Key Findings & Recommendations

Problem 1: Declining Productivity in Q3-Q4

Most programs show declining productivity from Q1 (high engagement) to Q4 (fatigue/disengagement). Average decline of 10-15% across all programs.

Action: Implement quarterly refresher modules instead of single intensive training. Spread training across year more evenly.

Problem 2: Mixed Effectiveness Results

60% programs effective/highly effective, but 30% show neutral results with declining trends, especially Leadership and Cybersecurity.

Action: Overhaul neutral programs with interactive, hands-on components. Focus on practical application vs theory.

Problem 3: Sustainability Gap

Strong initial impact (Q1 productivity 91-93%) but doesn't sustain. Employees retain only 60-70% of trained skills by year-end.

Action: Add ongoing reinforcement, mentoring programs, and performance follow-ups post-training.

Success Pattern: Large Cohorts Work Better

Programs with 150+ participants (Customer Experience, Leadership) show better ROI than small group training (5G Network: 59 participants).

Action: Consolidate training where possible to achieve economies of scale and group learning benefits.

Recommended Training Process Changes

1
Adopt Blended Learning Model

70% self-paced online + 20% instructor-led workshops + 10% peer learning/mentoring. Allows flexibility and better retention.

2
Implement Monthly Refresher Cycles

Instead of annual training, deploy monthly 2-hour reinforcement sessions to combat Q3-Q4 productivity decline.

3
Revamp Evaluation Method

Current measurement focuses on participation. Add pre/post assessments, 90-day performance tracking, and manager feedback.

4
Link L&D to Performance Ratings

Make training outcomes part of performance appraisal. This will increase accountability and skill application.

5
Prioritize Effective Programs

Double investment in Highly Effective programs (5G, Workplace Safety). Scale to larger audiences with lower per-participant cost.

6
Modernize Neutral Programs

Leadership and Cybersecurity need curriculum redesign with case studies, simulations, and real-world applications.

CONCLUSION: L&D Effectiveness Assessment

Does L&D improve performance? PARTIALLY YES

Current State: 60% of programs show strong ROI (Highly Effective/Effective), but sustainability is poor with 10-15% productivity decline by year-end.

Opportunity: Current training hours (34.93 hrs/employee) show moderate impact. With process improvements, estimated 25-30% improvement in performance outcomes.

Should Training Process Be Altered? YES - CRITICAL

Recommended Investment: ₹ 40 lakhs for L&D platform upgrade + ₹ 20 lakhs for curriculum revision + ₹ 15 lakhs for monthly reinforcement programs
